Project: Predictive Analytics for Diabetes Prevention
Objective: To identify the primary lifestyle and demographic drivers of diabetes to inform public health intervention strategies.

📌 Problem Statement
Diabetes is a growing global health crisis. However, many cases are preventable through lifestyle changes. By analyzing large-scale survey data, we can identify which specific behaviors (Physical Activity, Diet, Smoking, etc...) have the strongest statistical link to a diabetes diagnosis.

🎯 Project Objectives
Correlation Exploration: Determine which health indicators (Blood Pressure, Cholesterol, BMI, etc....) have the highest correlation with diabetes.

Risk Group Identification: Segment the population into "Low Risk" and "High Risk" based on lifestyle combinations.

Impact Analysis: Measure the percentage decrease in diabetes prevalence among physically active individuals compared to sedentary individuals.

Data Storytelling: Create a "Risk Profile" visualization that explains health risks to a non-medical audience.

🛠️ Tools Used
Python/Pandas: For large-scale data manipulation (250k+ rows).

Seaborn: For visualizing complex correlations.

NumPy: For efficient calculation of health ratios and risk percentages.
