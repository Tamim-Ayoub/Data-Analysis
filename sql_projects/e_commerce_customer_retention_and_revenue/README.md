### **PROJECT MANDATE: Operational Performance & Market Intelligence Audit**

**Document Type:** Strategic Objectives Definition

**Classification:** Internal Analytics Requirement

---

### **Overview**

The objective of this audit is to evaluate historical transaction data across the corporate database to extract key financial and behavioral metrics. The findings will be used to optimize inventory allocation, structure customer retention campaigns, and streamline regional logistics.

The following four specific business intelligence requirements must be extracted via database queries:

---

### **Requirement 1: Product Category Profitability Analysis**

* **Objective:** Evaluate the true financial contribution of each product category to identify high-margin segments versus low-margin, high-volume segments.
* **Data Required:** Total gross revenue and total net profit aggregated by product category, factoring in the differences between retail pricing and wholesale unit costs.

### **Requirement 2: High-Value Customer Identification**

* **Objective:** Isolate top-tier consumers to establish a roster for a targeted VIP loyalty and incentive program.
* **Data Required:** A list of customer profiles whose cumulative financial expenditure across all historic transactions exceeds a gross threshold of **$500**.

### **Requirement 3: Regional Demand Mapping (Electronics)**

* **Objective:** Determine the geographical origin of market demand within the high-ticket "Electronics" sector to optimize regional supply chain distribution.
* **Data Required:** A ranking of countries based on the total physical quantity of electronic units purchased.

### **Requirement 4: Logistical Cost & Freight Audit**

* **Objective:** Analyze the distribution of shipping costs across different business segments to identify logistics bottlenecks and categories with disproportionately high transport expenses.

* **Data Required:** The average (mean) freight value, maximum freight value, and the ratio of freight cost to product price, aggregated by product category.
